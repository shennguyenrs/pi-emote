# Multi-Frame Generation Prompts

These prompts are designed for **Image-to-Image** generation using the provided **Base Character** as the primary reference.

## Global Style Guide (System Prompt)

> "Maintain 100% consistency with the base character: **long pastel pink hair, large expressive brown eyes, black over-ear headphones, and a professional black suit with a white shirt and black tie.** The output must be 2 to 4 distinct frames in a single image (sprite sheet format), 128x128 pixel art resolution per frame. **The background must remain 100% identical and static across all frames:** a dark, stylish composition (charcoal or deep navy) featuring a subtle pixel-art checkerboard pattern and fixed decorative elements like tiny stars or soft neon glow. **Character size, height, and scale must remain perfectly consistent across all frames; she should not appear smaller or taller between transitions.** **Transitions between frames must be incremental and fluid.** In every frame, incorporate subtle manga/anime visual effects like small 'kira-kira' sparkles or floating hearts to ensure the character remains 'kawaii' (cute) throughout the animation. **Make various facial emotion and expression.**"

---

## 1. Idle & Blink (2 Frames)

**Goal:** A simple, charming blinking cycle.

- **Frame 1:** Neutral pose, eyes wide and sparkling with a 'kira-kira' effect.
- **Frame 2:** Eyes gently closed, a tiny 'smile' curve to the eyelids.
  **Prompt:**
  > "Generate 2 frames of the character: 1) Neutral pose with wide sparkling eyes and 'kira-kira' effects, 2) Eyes closed happily in a gentle blink. Maintain headphones, suit, character height, and the dark decorative background perfectly still."

---

## 2. Talk / Mouth Movement (4 Frames)

**Goal:** Smooth lip-sync with musical flair.

- **Frame 1:** Mouth closed in a small 'cat-like' smile.
- **Frame 2:** Mouth slightly open in a small 'o' shape.
- **Frame 3:** Mouth moderately open with a floating pink music note.
- **Frame 4:** Mouth wide open in a happy 'D' shape with tiny sparkles inside.
  **Prompt:**
  > "Generate 4 frames of mouth movement: 1) Small closed smile, 2) Tiny 'o' mouth, 3) Moderate 'a' mouth with a floating music note, 4) Wide happy 'D' mouth with tiny sparkles. The dark background, character scale, and position must remain identical across all frames."

- **Frame 4:** 'Aha!' moment: wide eyes, finger raised, surrounded by bright stars and 'kira-kira' effects.

## 5. Write (4 Frames)

**Goal:** Graceful writing with magical trails.

- **Frame 1:** Holding a stylus ready near the chin, looking focused.
- **Frame 2:** Stylus tip touching the "paper" area, eyes looking down, a faint trail of pink light.
- **Frame 3:** **(Same pose as Frame 2)** Intense focus, more manga stars and light trails 'popping' out.
- **Frame 4:** **(Same pose as Frame 2)** Looking at the camera with a happy wink and a 'finished' flower effect.
  **Prompt:**
  > "Generate 4 frames of writing: 1) Focused, stylus ready near chin, 2) Writing with eyes looking down and a pink light trail, 3) Same writing pose with intense popping manga stars, 4) Same writing pose but looking at camera with a wink and 'flower' success effect. Keep character scale, arm position, and background 100% identical across frames 2, 3, and 4."

## 9. Compact (4 Frames)

**Goal:** A cute "compacting" or "shrinking" animation.

- **Frame 1:** Squatting down with a playful smile, preparing to "shrink".
- **Frame 2:** Curling into a tight, cute ball and holding her knees.
- **Frame 3:** Surrounded by a stylized 'box' or 'zip' graphic with sparkles.
- **Frame 4:** Transformed into a tiny, simplified 'mini' version of herself waving happily.
  **Prompt:**
  > "Generate 4 frames of 'compact' animation: 1) Character squatting down playfully, 2) Curled into a cute ball, 3) Surrounded by a stylized manga box/zip graphic with sparkles, 4) A tiny simplified mini-character waving with 'kira-kira' effects. Keep the dark background elements and global scale consistent."
