# Multi-Frame Generation Prompts

These prompts are designed for **Image-to-Image** generation using the provided **Base Character** as the primary reference.

## Global Style Guide (System Prompt)
> "Maintain 100% consistency with the base character: **long pastel pink hair, large expressive brown eyes, black over-ear headphones, and a professional black suit with a white shirt and black tie.** The output must be 4 distinct frames in a single image (sprite sheet format), 1024x1024 resolution per frame, transparent background. **Transitions between frames must be incremental and fluid.** In every frame, incorporate subtle manga/anime visual effects like small 'kira-kira' sparkles, floating hearts, or stylized emote symbols to ensure the character remains 'kawaii' (cute) throughout the animation."

---

## 1. Idle & Blink (2 Frames)
**Goal:** A simple, charming blinking cycle.
*   **Frame 1:** Neutral pose, eyes wide and sparkling with a 'kira-kira' effect.
*   **Frame 2:** Eyes gently closed, a tiny 'smile' curve to the eyelids.
**Prompt:**
> "Generate 2 frames of the character: 1) Neutral pose with wide sparkling eyes and 'kira-kira' effects, 2) Eyes closed happily in a gentle blink. Maintain headphones and suit perfectly still."

---

## 2. Talk / Mouth Movement (4 Frames)
**Goal:** Smooth lip-sync with musical flair.
*   **Frame 1:** Mouth closed in a small 'cat-like' smile.
*   **Frame 2:** Mouth slightly open in a small 'o' shape.
*   **Frame 3:** Mouth moderately open with a floating pink music note.
*   **Frame 4:** Mouth wide open in a happy 'D' shape with tiny sparkles inside.
**Prompt:**
> "Generate 4 frames of mouth movement: 1) Small closed smile, 2) Tiny 'o' mouth, 3) Moderate 'a' mouth with a floating music note, 4) Wide happy 'D' mouth with tiny sparkles. Ensure eyes remain wide and cute."

## 3. Think / Pondering (4 Frames)
**Goal:** A cute, intellectual progression.

*   **Frame 2:** Tapping chin with finger, a small '?' symbol appearing.
*   **Frame 3:** Eyes closed, head tilted slightly, a small '...' bubble.
*   **Frame 4:** 'Aha!' moment: wide eyes, finger raised, surrounded by bright stars and 'kira-kira' effects.
**Prompt:**
> "Generate 4 frames of thinking: 1) Looking up-left, finger near chin, 2) Tapping chin with '?' symbol, 3) Eyes closed in thought with '...' bubble, 4) 'Aha!' realization with wide eyes and bright manga stars."

## 9. Compact (4 Frames)
**Goal:** A cute "compacting" or "shrinking" animation.
*   **Frame 1:** Squatting down with a playful smile, preparing to "shrink".
*   **Frame 2:** Curling into a tight, cute ball and holding her knees.
*   **Frame 3:** Surrounded by a stylized 'box' or 'zip' graphic with sparkles.
*   **Frame 4:** Transformed into a tiny, simplified 'mini' version of herself waving happily.
**Prompt:**
> "Generate 4 frames of 'compact' animation: 1) Character squatting down playfully, 2) Curled into a cute ball, 3) Surrounded by a stylized manga box/zip graphic with sparkles, 4) A tiny simplified mini-character waving with 'kira-kira' effects."
